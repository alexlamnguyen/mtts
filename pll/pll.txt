f_vco = f_pd * PLL_N_PRE * PLL_N
	using reference f doubler, f_pd = 200MHz
	prescaler set to 2 or 4, use 2 for lower noise
	PLL_N = 14

phase noise: random fluctuations in phase
flat in-band noise: phase noise by PLL (aka PLL noise floor)
open-loop noise: phase noise of VCO running free
jitter: how much signal edges deviate from timing
	integrate phase noise
flicker noise: power spectral density inversely proportional to f
	drops off at high offset frequency (louder around 5.6GHz)
	(aka 1/f noise)
offset frequency: distance of measured frequency from real 5.6GHz signal
	corner frequency: where downward slope of flicker noise intersects flat line of noise floor
		below corner: flicker noise dominates signal
